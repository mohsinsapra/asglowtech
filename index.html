Questions

Before you order

Do I pay anything now?

No. Cash on delivery is the only way to pay here. You hand the money to the rider when the bulbs reach you — nothing is charged online, and the site never asks for card details.

How long does delivery take?

Two to four working days to Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan and Peshawar. Up to six working days elsewhere. Abdullah confirms the timing on WhatsApp when he takes the order.

What if a bulb arrives broken or dead?

Send a photo on WhatsApp and it's replaced free. The one-year warranty covers a bulb that stops working in normal use — it doesn't cover a bulb dropped on the floor.

Will these fit my holder?

Every bulb here is an E27 screw fitting for 220-volt mains, which is the standard round-threaded holder in most Pakistani homes. If yours is the small bayonet push-and-twist type, message Abdullah before ordering.

Can I order for a shop or a whole house?

Yes. For twenty bulbs or more, message Abdullah on WhatsApp for wholesale pricing rather than ordering through the cart.
